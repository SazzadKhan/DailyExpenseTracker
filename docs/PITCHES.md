# Daily Expense Tracker — Pitch Deck (Pen-Selling Edition)

Four pitches. Same product. Different angle for different audiences.
Pick one based on who's in the room.

---

## Pitch 1 — The Detective Story
*Audience: anyone who's ever stared at a bank statement going "...what."*

> *"Sell me this pen."*
> *"Sure — but first, do you know where your last $47 went?"*

Your bank app tells you you're broke.
Your bank app does **not** tell you why.

Every month, money walks out of your account like it's late for a meeting,
and you're left doing forensics with a coffee cup full of receipts.
DoorDash, Amazon, a "miscellaneous" $19 charge from a company you swear
you've never heard of. You play detective. You lose.

**This app is the case file.**

- One tap to log a spend. No accounts. No bank linking. No 47-page T&Cs.
- Your bank says `DOORDASH $34.12`. We say **🍔 Food → Takeout, 4th time this week, you've spent more on burritos than your phone bill.**
- Charts that show you the *story*, not just the numbers.
- Your data lives in *your* browser or *your* Google Sheet. Not ours. There's nothing for us to leak.

> "So… do I buy it?"
> "It's free. You already 'bought' it. Now go log that coffee." ☕

## Pitch 2 — The Gym Membership Analogy
*Audience: skeptics who've tried (and abandoned) ten finance apps.*

You know what's wild?

You'll spend **$60/month** on a gym you visit twice
to fix a body you can see in the mirror —
but you won't spend **30 seconds a day** on a tracker
to fix a bank account you *can't* see at all.

We're not asking for $60. We're not asking for $6. We're not asking for $0.99.
**We're asking for the 30 seconds.**

Here's the deal:

| Other apps | This one |
|---|---|
| Sign up flow | Open. Use. |
| "Connect your bank" | No connections, ever |
| Free tier locked behind "Pro" | Everything's free, forever |
| Your data lives on their server | Your data lives in *your* Google Drive |
| Monthly subscription | Monthly nothing |
| 6 dark patterns to cancel | There's nothing to cancel |

The hardest part of any habit isn't doing it. It's *starting* it.
We removed every reason to not start.

You don't need motivation. You need a button.
**There is a button.** It says `+ Log Entry`. Press it.

## Pitch 3 — The Therapist Pitch
*Audience: anyone who has Money Feelings™.*

Most expense trackers are built like accountants.
This one's built like a friend who's seen your spending and isn't going to make it weird.

There's no:
- ❌ Red warning when you "go over"
- ❌ Streak you can break
- ❌ Guilt notification
- ❌ "You spent $200 on coffee this month 😬" judgment screen
- ❌ Annoying paywalls right when you're trying to be responsible

There *is*:
- ✅ A budget bar that quietly shows where you are
- ✅ Four themes (yes, including **Edgy** — for when paying rent feels personal)
- ✅ Subtle haptics, soft tap sounds, little confetti when you log
- ✅ Charts that just… *show* you. No commentary.

**The point isn't to shame you into spending less.**
The point is to *see* what you're already doing.
Awareness without anxiety. Once you can see it, you'll make better calls on your own.
You don't need a stern app. You need a clear one.

## Pitch 4 — The "I'm Sus Of Your Data" Pitch
*Audience: privacy nerds, devs, anyone who's been burned by a data leak.*

Quick quiz:

1. Where does Mint store your data? *(They got shut down. Where did it go?)*
2. Where does YNAB store your data? *(Their servers. Their schema. Their backups.)*
3. Where does this app store your data?

**Answer 3:** Your browser. Or, if you sign in, **a Google Sheet on YOUR Google Drive that this app creates for you.**

- We don't have a database. There's nothing for us to lose.
- We don't have a backend. There's nothing for us to compromise.
- We don't have an account system. There's no password to leak.
- We don't have analytics. We literally don't know you exist.
- The whole app is a static page. You can read every line of source code.
- You can export your data to CSV any time. You can also nuke it from the settings menu, and it's actually gone.

The OAuth scope is **a single Google Sheet, on your own Drive, that this app creates**.
We don't see your other files. We don't see your other sheets.
Revoke access any time from your Google account — and the worst thing that happens is you can't sync anymore.

> **"The best privacy policy is having nothing to take."**
> We took that literally.

## Universal Closer (works after any of the above)

You don't need another app.
You need to **see your money** — clearly, privately, without a subscription, without an account, without a *journey*.

Open the page. Add three expenses. That's the demo.
By minute two, you'll already know something about your spending you didn't know this morning.

That's the pitch. That's the pen. That's the product.
